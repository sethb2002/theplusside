How to read it: Edge is the model's chance minus the chance the odds imply. +3.0 means the model thinks the player is 3 points more likely to score first than the price suggests. Per $10 is the average profit or loss on a $10 bet if the model is right. Sportsbooks build a margin into these prices, so most players will show as overpriced; that's normal. Not betting advice.
